Graphic Systems & Packaging

Information Design · Packaging System

Visual Codes for Milk

A visual identification system that reimagines how shoppers compare milk products at a glance. The project turns nutritional information into a repeatable visual language designed for faster, more intuitive decisions in a grocery aisle.

Color identifies product type, dot density represents fat content, a ring indicator communicates protein level, and carton fullness estimates calories across seven milk varieties.

Role: Research, concept development, information design, illustration, packaging-system design, typography, and layout.

Campaign Design

Museum Promotion · Poster Campaign

Eye on Conservation

A promotional poster campaign for the Georgia Museum of Natural History that encourages visitors to engage with conservation-focused collections. The campaign uses simplified animal illustrations, bold typography, and a restricted green-and-cream palette to create a cohesive visual system across multiple poster formats.

Role: Campaign concept, visual design, illustration, typography, layout, and promotional communication.
